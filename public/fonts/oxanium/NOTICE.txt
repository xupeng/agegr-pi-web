Oxanium webfonts
================

Two variable webfont files covering the `Oxanium` family, weights 300-700:

  oxanium-latin-wght-normal.woff2      (latin subset)
  oxanium-latin-ext-wght-normal.woff2  (latin-ext subset)

Source: @fontsource-variable/oxanium@5.3.0
  https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@fontsource-variable/oxanium@5.3.0/files/
Upstream project: https://github.com/sevmeyer/oxanium
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 -- see LICENSE-oxanium.txt in this directory.

These files are byte-identical to the `url(...)` targets of the Google Fonts
CSS that `app/layout.tsx` used to link from fonts.googleapis.com.
